```
{district_name}, Maharashtra — {pincode}
```

```
    श्रीमती कांबळे या BPL कुटुंबातील असून, त्यांचे सध्याचे कच्चे (मातीचे)
    घर पावसाळ्यात राहण्यायोग्य राहत नाही. पती निधनानंतर त्या एकट्या

    मंजुरी प्राप्त झालेली नाही.

    मिळवून देणे हे शासनाचे कर्तव्य आहे.

    तरी श्रीमती अनिता कांबळे यांचा प्रधानमंत्री आवास योजना अर्ज
```

**Scenario:** Land dispute resolution request

```
Outward No.: OUT/2026/0091                               Date: 02/08/2026

Ref: 1) Inward No. INW/2026/0055, Dated 28/07/2026

To,
Respected District Collector,
Shri. Amit Kulkarni,
District Collector's Office,
Pune — 411001.

Subject: Land Boundary Dispute — Gat No. 145, Mauze Ambegaon — Request for Survey
         and Resolution

Respected Sir,

    I am writing to bring to your kind attention that Shri. Ramesh Patil and
    Shri. Ganesh More, both residents of Mauze Ambegaon, Haveli Taluka, have
    been engaged in a prolonged land boundary dispute over Gat No. 145.
    The matter has been pending resolution for over two years.

    The dispute has created significant tension in the village community and has
    disrupted agricultural activities for both families. Multiple attempts at
    local resolution through the Gram Panchayat have been unsuccessful. Both
    parties have now approached this office requesting official intervention.

    I therefore request you to direct the concerned Revenue Officer to conduct
    an official land survey of Gat No. 145 and issue a formal boundary order
    at the earliest. Kindly inform this office of the action taken.

                                              Yours faithfully,


                                              Hon. Rajesh Sharma
                                              MLA, Haveli Vidhan Sabha Constituency
                                              Tel: 020-25512345

CC:
1) Tahsildar, Haveli Taluka
2) Gram Panchayat, Ambegaon
```
